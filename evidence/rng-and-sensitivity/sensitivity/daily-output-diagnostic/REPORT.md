# Daily-output diagnostic from existing 500-ED probes

These are three already accepted single-seed probes, not ten independent daily repetitions and not new cells of the sensitivity design. Daily generation cohorts are observed through the 12-day horizon; final-day censoring remains explicit.

| Stage | Daily cohort PDR range (%) | Daily energy/node range (J) |

| F3 | 27.6311–28.0792 | 2.006113–2.056382 |
| F4 | 23.5629–24.0186 | 1.711562–1.788079 |

The daily PDR, RF transmission and energy series over days 2–12 do not show a large persistent decline in these examples. This supports examining a post-two-day output window in these individual probes, but does not prove stationarity, validity for all seeds, or the adequacy of two days for every treatment.

In particular, permanently blocked MAC nodes continue accumulating application demand. Nearly constant aggregate delivery/energy rates can coexist with growing queues: output-rate stability is not queue-state equilibrium. The new 0/1/2/3-day warm-up conditions quantify robustness of the paired finite-horizon contrasts separately.

Every daily generation and delivery sum and the clipped state-energy total reconcile with the accepted item-2 accounting summary. Source hashes are recorded in status.json; those full traces are delivered with item 2.
