# Attribution and licensing boundaries

Enhanced FLoRa is derived from FLoRa by Michał Słabicki and contributors.
The reference release is v1.1.0, commit
51018f80fdafc562d8f9fa022e551f835c8969ee.

The Enhanced research authors are Juan P. Morales-Alvarez, Víktor I.
Rodríguez-Abdalá, Remberto Sandoval-Arechiga, Salvador Ibarra-Delgado and
José Ricardo Gómez-Rodríguez. Attribution to them concerns their contributions,
not authorship of the original simulator or its dependencies.

1. FLoRa-derived software retains its LGPLv3-or-later notices. Existing
   third-party/per-file notices take precedence over a directory-level summary.
2. The authors' experiment outputs and newly authored documentation are
   offered under CC BY 4.0. Original license texts, third-party documents,
   simulator dependencies and the manuscript are excluded from that grant.
3. OMNeT++ uses its own Academic Public License; INET retains its own
   GPL/LGPL and per-file notices. Neither is relicensed as CC BY or MIT.
4. Source/configuration copies from rc11 are byte-preserving. The new packaging
   and verification utilities are dated 2026-11-11; they do not change the
   historical simulator or pretend to be its original build log.
5. The public redistribution omits manuscript directories, including their
   copies inside nested archives. Production code, configuration and test
   outputs retain their original bytes. `PUBLICATION_FILTER.json` distinguishes
   the original source archive identity from the filtered packaging hashes.
   Technical verification reports are retained; research-paper drafts are not.

No endorsement by the upstream FLoRa authors is implied.
